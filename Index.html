THE PROBLEM WE FACE

Long-term residents
are being priced out of
the communities they built.

For decades, families in Calaveras, Amador, and Tuolumne counties have lived, worked, raised children, and supported local businesses. These "Residins" — multi-generational residents with deep roots — are the backbone of the Mother Lode.

Surging demand, outside investment, and limited housing supply have driven prices dramatically higher. Long-time residents on fixed incomes and young families inheriting homes are facing impossible choices: sell the only home they’ve known, or leave the community they helped build.

What “Residin” Means

10–50+ years of continuous residence
Built local institutions, businesses, and civic life
Multi-generational families who want to stay
Now facing displacement from market forces they didn’t create
